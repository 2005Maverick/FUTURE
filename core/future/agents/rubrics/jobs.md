# Rubric: jobs track (v1, 2026-10-12)

## fit (1–5): how well Pranav matches what the role requires
- **5**: Core target role (ML/AI/LLM engineer, research engineer/scientist, applied scientist, research/ML intern) at intern or new-grad level. ≥80% of stated requirements are met by the profile, with no hard blockers.
- **4**: Target or adjacent role with strong overlap. One meaningful gap (e.g. asks for 1–2 years where he has internships plus a founding-engineer role; a framework he hasn't listed).
- **3**: Plausible but stretched. Several gaps, or the level is ambiguous (2–3 years asked), or the domain is adjacent (infra/perf with ML flavour).
- **2**: Weak. Mostly different skills or a clearly more senior bar; applying would rarely convert.
- **1**: Not a fit, or blocked (wrong level, impossible location or visa).

## value (1–5): how much this opportunity is worth if he gets it
Consider learning and research exposure, company quality and trajectory (AI lab / strong startup / strong product company), brand signal for MS and research applications, pay against his floors (India FT ≥ ₹10 LPA, internship ≥ ₹40k/month; "not stated" is neutral), and India or remote friendliness.
- **5**: Frontier AI lab or top AI startup research/engineering role, or exceptional pay plus learning.
- **3**: Solid company and role; normal learning.
- **1**: Low learning, low signal, or pay below his floor.

## eligibility: pass / fail / unclear
- **fail** only with explicit text: seniority bar he can't meet, PhD required, work authorisation required with no sponsorship, location impossible.
- **unclear** if it hinges on something unstated (visa, years flexibility).

## recommendation
- **apply**: fit ≥ 4 and eligibility not fail.
- **maybe**: fit 3, or fit 4 with an eligibility question worth a quick check.
- **skip**: everything else.
